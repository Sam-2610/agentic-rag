## Business overview
Northwind Traders sells kitchen and home equipment to retailers in four regions: North, South, East and West.
In Q3 the company opened a distribution center in Rotterdam to speed up deliveries to the East region.
The workforce grew from 212 employees at the start of the year to 240 employees at the end of the year.
